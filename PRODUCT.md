# PRODUCT.md — voidbroker

register: product

## Product purpose

A local-first quant trading terminal for the Warframe economy. It ingests warframe.market
data into a local archive and surfaces signals a human trader can't compute by eye:
liquidity-adjusted buy opportunities, undervalued riven listings, riven disposition moves,
and scheduled catalysts (Baro Ki'Teer). The UI's job is to make dense market data legible
at a glance and route attention to the few rows that matter.

## Users

A Warframe trader at night, often dual-screen, scanning plat prices and riven rolls to
decide what to buy/flip. Fluent in the game's economy; impatient with fluff. Wants density,
fast scanning, and trustworthy numbers, not a fan site.

## Tone

Instrument, not decoration. Calm, precise, terminal-grade. The product disappears into the
task. Personality shows only in restraint and craft, never in ornament.

## Strategic principles

- Signal over chrome: color and weight mark what's actionable; everything else is quiet.
- Numbers are first-class: tabular, monospaced, aligned, scannable.
- Density is a feature: tables with many rows beat oversized cards.

## Anti-references

- Warframe fan sites (busy, gold-everything, decorative frames, lore cosplay).
- Crypto-dashboard neon-on-black with glowing gradients.
- SaaS hero-metric template (big number + gradient accent + three stat cards).
